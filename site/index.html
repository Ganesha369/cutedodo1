🐶 The real dog behind $DODO

No stock photos.
Just Dodo.

In a small home in Tamil Nadu...

A fluffy little Shih Tzu named Dodo joined VJDurga's family and filled every corner with love. Kids play with him, grandma sits beside him, and every visitor falls for that smile.

Seeing how much joy one small dog can bring, the family and close friends decided to do something bigger. $DODO is not for quick pumps. It is for a long-term mission: water for thirsty street dogs, food for hungry ones, and shelter for those who have none.

Real, doxxed dog lovers from different countries stand behind it, connected through VJDurga's journey across 7 nations and web3. Guardian wallets never sell. Donation wallets show every bit of help that reaches the dogs.

What is CuteDoDo ($DODO)?

A community meme token on Solana inspired by Dodo, a real Shih Tzu from Chennai, Tamil Nadu. It turns the joy of a family pet into real help for street dogs: water, meals, vet care and shelter support.

Where can I buy it?

On pump.fun or any Solana DEX through Jupiter. Always double-check the contract address: 2e5bKRHpAixvgE6zUdTf7rigxCq662zq7xvN8Dympump.

Is there a buy or sell tax?

No. $DODO has 0% buy and 0% sell tax. You only pay the platform's normal swap fee and Solana network gas.

How does the charity work?

A public charity wallet holds 1.15% of supply. Donations go out at market-cap milestones, holders vote on the shelters, and every transfer is posted with Solscan proof and photos.

What are Guardian wallets?

10 wallets held by long-term pack members across India, Chile, Uruguay, the UK and the USA, together 8.75% of supply. They are public and committed to never selling.

What is a Liquid NFT?

The name for planned Dodo collectibles on Solana that come with membership perks and, later, ways to trade them more easily. They are not issued yet. Details are in the whitepaper.

Is my wallet safe when I connect?

This site never asks for your seed phrase or private keys and never holds your funds. Your wallet signs every trade. If anyone asks for your seed phrase, it is a scam.
